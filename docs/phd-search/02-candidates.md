## Band A — go

### Vienna

**Markus Aspelmeyer** — Universität Wien / IQOQI Vienna. **[✓]**
Founded quantum optomechanics as a field. Three-time ERC grantee; **EPS Prize for
Fundamental Aspects 2025**; **Wittgenstein Prize, June 2026, €2M** — Austria's highest
individual research award. Current work: levitated optomechanics, magnetically levitated
superconductors coupled to microwave circuits, and the long programme toward putting a
massive object in superposition. Entirely table-top.
*Why this one matters:* it is the strongest combination of PI calibre and city on this
list. Vienna already scored highest of any large city on the life criteria, and
Baltuška is at TU Wien in the same city with a live referral channel through Ömer.

**Andrius Baltuška** — TU Wien, Photonics Institute. **[~]**
Ultrafast and nonlinear optics, builds his own sources. Already on the target list.

### Berlin

**Arno Rauschenbeutel** — HU Berlin. **[✓]**
Founded chiral quantum optics and nanofibre waveguide QED. Alexander von Humboldt
Professorship (up to €5M); **ERC Synergy "SuperWave", >€8M** with Hofferberth (Bonn) and
Pohl (Aarhus). Output: PRX 14, 011020 (2024); PRL 136, 063402 (2026); PRL 128, 073601
(2022). Cold atoms coupled to self-pulled tapered nanofibres — optical table, UHV
chamber, nothing else. Zero facility dependence, the cleanest pass on filter 2 of anyone
here.

**Nathalie Picqué** — MBI, Division C. **[~]**
Founding-level in dual-comb spectroscopy, Hänsch lineage. Builds her own combs.
Division explicitly invites unsolicited applications.

**Tobias Kampfrath** — FU Berlin / FHI. **[✓]**
Established THz spintronics as a field. **ERC Advanced "ORBITERA", €2.5M (2024)**, on
terahertz orbitronics. The group's spintronic THz emitters are now used well beyond his
own lab. THz time-domain detection is field-resolved detection.

### Barcelona

**Niek van Hulst** — ICFO. **[✓]**
ICREA Research Professor, ERC Advanced. Single-molecule ultrafast nano-optics: fs
coherent control on single emitters, nanoantennas, phase-controlled detection of energy
transfer in light-harvesting complexes. Ultrafast meets ultrasmall, all on a table.

**Jens Biegert** — ICFO. **[?]**
Mid-IR OPCPA, attosecond, table-top soft-X-ray HHG. The most direct lineage to your
existing background of anyone in band A — Ugaitz Elu in Berlin is his student. Verify he
is still taking students.

### Hamburg

**Andrea Cavalleri** — MPSD. **[✓]**
Light-induced superconductivity: mid-IR pulses driving atomic modes to stabilise
superconducting fluctuations above equilibrium T_c (YBCO, K₃C₆₀, κ-(ET)₂X). Among the
most conceptually ambitious experimental programmes in Europe, and mid-IR driven.
*Check:* part of the group's probing uses FEL/X-ray facilities. The pump–probe optics is
table-top; ask for the split.

### Regensburg

**Rupert Huber** — Universität Regensburg, chair of physics. **[✓]**
Lightwave electronics — using the oscillating field of light to move charge faster than
one optical cycle. ERC Consolidator. Subcycle near-field optics, HHG in solids,
femtosecond STM at atomic resolution. Table-top, and intellectually exactly the kind of
"what does the field itself do" physics you are after.
*Cost:* Regensburg, ~150k.

### Rostock

**Eleftherios Goulielmakis** — Universität Rostock, Extreme Photonics. **[✓]**
ERC Advanced. Optical attosecond pulses and "picoscopy"; his group produced 53-attosecond
electron pulses from a tungsten nanotip. Table-top attosecond, source-building at the
limit. *Cost:* Rostock is small and not cosmopolitan.

### Erlangen

**Peter Hommelhoff** — FAU Erlangen, Chair for Laser Physics. **[✓]**
Steering electrons with phase-controlled few-cycle pulses — at needle tips, in graphene,
and in dielectric laser accelerators. Lightwave electronics and electron quantum optics.
Table-top; the electron source is built, not bought.

**Hanieh Fattahi** — MPL Erlangen. **[~]** · **Birgit Stiller** — MPL Erlangen. **[~]**
Already the two best scientific matches found. Unchanged.

### Zurich

**Lukas Novotny** — ETH Zürich, Photonics Laboratory. **[✓]**
Levitodynamics: optically levitated nanoparticles, ground-state cooling of multiple
modes, force sensing below zero-point fluctuations. **ERC Synergy "Q-Xtreme"** — putting
a 100-nm object into superposition. Co-author of *Principles of Nano-Optics*.
*Cost:* Zurich was rated a non-match on the life criteria. Listed because the filter
says list it; the city trade is yours.

### Paris / Palaiseau

**Antoine Browaeys** — Laboratoire Charles Fabry, Institut d'Optique, CNRS. **[✓]**
Founding-level in Rydberg tweezer arrays: defect-free arrays of up to 200 single atoms,
full geometric control in 1/2/3D, engineered spin Hamiltonians. Table-top.
*Cost:* Palaiseau is on the Saclay plateau — the Paris commute problem.

### Amsterdam

**Ewold Verhagen** — AMOLF **[~]** · **Said Rodriguez** — AMOLF **[~]**
Unchanged from the earlier survey. Both still band A.

## Out

Only these, and only for filter 1 or 2:

- **PIC / integrated photonics** — Mørk, Oxenløwe, Hu (DTU), Errando-Herranz (Delft),
  Melloni, Bogoni (Italy), and TU Berlin's CIPHOR cluster. Your call, stands.
- **Cleanroom-dominated** — Reitzenstein, Kneissl, Koblmüller, Schröder (TU/HU Berlin).
- **External large facilities** — Eisebitt (BESSY/PETRA/XFEL/FERMI/LCLS), Nikhef,
  anything ELI-based.
- **Bought electron/X-ray instrument as the central tool** — Polman, Ernstorfer,
  Lehmann, Albrecht, Kanngießer.

Note the distinction that replaces the old "no electron microscopy" rule: what matters
is whether the instrument is built or bought. Hommelhoff's electron source is built and
he is band A; a commercial TEM with a laser bolted on is not the same thing.
